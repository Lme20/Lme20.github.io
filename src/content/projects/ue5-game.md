A small game I made in Unreal Engine 5 in 2022. Most of the gameplay is built with UE's Blueprint visual scripting system.

## What I built

- **Score system**: a kill counter that updates as enemies die.
- **Combat system**: enemy NPCs that the player fights.
- **Life bars and death**: health tracked as a life bar, with death handled when it runs out.

I also designed the UI myself (portrait and health bar, kill counter, minimap, controls panel) and built a playable map. Alongside these, I've used premade assets for the terrain, player and NPC models, this includes shaders and textures.
