Group project at the University of Gothenburg (5 members). We designed a high-fidelity Figma prototype for university students to track mood, activity and study performance, and evaluated it with 10 students through usability testing and a post-trial survey.

I led the overall design of the concept and UI, deciding how the interface should look and how each feature translated into UI components.

Emoji-based mood input was the highest-rated feature (4.5/5). Finding the AI insights page was the main usability problem.
